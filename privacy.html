Privacy Policy — Vaya

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Overview

Vaya (“we”, “us”, or “our”) provides a platform for users to discover and join real-world activities in their local area.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Vaya mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

a. Account Information

  • Email address
  • Username (if provided)

b. Location Information

To enable core functionality, we collect approximate or precise location data (if permitted by you).

This is used to:

  • Show nearby activities
  • Allow others to discover your activity

c. User Content

  • Activity posts (title, description, location, time)
  • Profile information
  • Messages and interactions with other users

d. Usage Data

  • App interactions
  • Device type
  • Logs and diagnostics

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the app
  • Show relevant nearby activities
  • Enable users to join and interact
  • Improve product performance and experience
  • Ensure safety and prevent misuse

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data.

a. With Other Users

  • Your activity posts and profile may be visible to nearby users
  • Your username may be visible when you join activities

b. With Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate the app, including:

  • Supabase (authentication, database, storage)

c. For Safety and Legal Reasons

  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect user safety
  • Prevent fraud or abuse

5. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

6. Your Choices

  • Control location permissions via your device settings
  • Stop using the app at any time
  • Request deletion of your account by contacting us

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no system is completely secure.

8. Children

Vaya is not intended for users under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction).

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of updates.
