FAQ.

Who can message me?

Direct messages only open after two people accept a connection request. Event chat opens only after you join the same plan.

How far ahead can I post a plan?

Up to about 36 hours. Vaya is for what's happening soon, not what's happening in October.

Who can see my exact location?

Nobody. Plans use a public meetup point you choose; we never share your live device location.

Do I have to share my phone number?

No. Sign in with Apple, Google, or email.

What if a plan goes badly?

Leave the chat, block the person, and rate the plan privately. We review reports and can remove accounts.

Is there an Android version?

A cross-platform version is in development. iOS is shipping first.
